The Gap

AI adoption is outrunning AI governance.

Copilot licences get switched on. Models get connected to live data. Meanwhile the questions that actually carry risk — who approved this, whose data is in it, what happens if it's wrong — are often answered after the fact, if at all.

 
01 Vendor AI tools go live with no cultural or legal review of the data feeding them.
02

Māori and Indigenous data sits inside systems with no rangatiratanga, no consent trail, no way back.

03

Boards ask "are we compliant?" and nobody owns a straight answer.

The Foundation

Six principles, non-negotiable.

Every recommendation in the framework is tested against Te Mana Raraunga's principles for Māori Data Sovereignty (2018) before it's tested against anything else — including the Microsoft roadmap.

Rangatiratanga
Authority

Māori hold decision-making authority over Māori data — what's collected, how it's used, and by whom.

Whakapapa
Relationships

Data carries its origins with it. Provenance and context travel with every dataset and model output.

Whanaungatanga
Obligations

Individual data rights are weighed against the rights of the collective they belong to.

Kotahitanga
Collective benefit

Systems are designed so Māori communities gain real, tangible benefit — not just the organisation.

Manaakitanga
Reciprocity

Dignity and consent come first. Free, prior and informed consent is a design requirement, not a checkbox.

Kaitiakitanga
Guardianship

Tikanga and mātauranga Māori shape how data is protected, accessed, and used — end to end.
